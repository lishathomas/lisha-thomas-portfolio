About

I'm Lisha Thomas, a UX/UI Designer based in Dubai, with a background that might surprise you — I studied Architecture.

That foundation changed how I approach design. Architecture taught me to think in systems, to consider how people move through spaces, and to always ask why before asking how. I carry that same thinking into every app and interface I design.

I transitioned into UX through a Google UX Design certification on Coursera, and since then I've been building projects that solve real friction points — from pizza ordering flows to personal finance tools. I work end-to-end: research, wireframes, design systems, and prototypes in Figma.

What drives me is the moment when something clicks for a user — when an interface gets out of their way and just works.

What I bring to a team:

  • Structured thinking from an architecture background
  • End-to-end design process from research to hi-fi prototype
  • A genuine curiosity for how people actually behave, not just how we assume they do

When I'm not designing, I'm sketching or deep in a Figma file experimenting with micro-interactions.
