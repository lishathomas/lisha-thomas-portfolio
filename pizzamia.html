Pizzamia

Mobile App  ·  UX / UI Design

Pizzamia is a pizza ordering mobile app designed to make the experience of ordering your favourite pizza fast, delightful, and effortless. The project focused on building an intuitive flow from browsing to checkout, with a visual language that feels warm and appetising.

UX / UI Designer
iOS Mobile App
Figma
Personal Project

The Problem

Most pizza ordering apps feel cluttered and transactional — long menus, confusing customisation flows, and checkout processes that create unnecessary friction. Users often abandon their order midway due to a lack of visual clarity and feedback.

The goal was to design an experience that feels as good as the pizza itself: clean, fast, and satisfying from first tap to order confirmation.

Design Process

I started with a competitive audit of popular food delivery apps, identifying pain points in the ordering flow. User flows were mapped to understand where drop-offs typically happen, which informed the structure of the navigation and the checkout steps.

Low-fidelity wireframes were tested with quick user walkthroughs before moving into high-fidelity UI. The visual design prioritised warm tones, generous whitespace, and mouth-watering photography to drive appetite appeal.
